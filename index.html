About

"Every garden holds a story the rain has already begun to tell."

Evie Fawbush grew up in Minnesota surronded by a loving family and friend group who has supported her throught her creative journey. Ever since she was a child she has loved the art of storytelling and has often found comfort inbetween the pages of a good book.

Evie wants to impower others through her writing, giving people a safe space like many authors have for her. She loves baking, being outside, and reading when she isn't at her dance studio, school, or writing.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Where does your inspiration come from?
My writing is rooted in close observation of the natural world—seasonal rhythms, the lives of plants and animals, and the places I've lived. I'm drawn to the slow unfolding of things and the stories embedded in soil and seasons.
Can I invite you to speak at my event?
I'm always interested in events that align with my work. Please reach out through the contact form with details about your event, and we can discuss possibilities.
Do you accept submissions for anthologies or collaborations?
I consider anthology and collaboration opportunities on a case-by-case basis. Please contact me with specifics about your project, timeline, and vision.
Where can I buy your books?
My books are available through major retailers including Amazon, Apple Books, and independent bookstores. Contact your local bookstore to order, or reach out if you're seeking signed copies.
